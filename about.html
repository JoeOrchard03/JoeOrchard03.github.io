About me

Hello! I am Joe Orchard I’ve been passionate about computers and video games from a young age, starting with basic programming tools like Scratch in primary school. Through secondary school and my computer science classes, I discovered a strong interest in programming, combined with my love of video games I knew game development and software development was what I wanted to pursue.

Since then, I’ve built up my programming and development skills using a range of tools and engines. I focused on Unreal Engine during college and have worked extensively with Unity at university.

I thrive in multi-disciplinary teams and have enjoyed collaborating with a diverse group of developers, artists, and designers during my time at Falmouth University. I’m excited to continue working with creative and ambitious teams to bring exciting projects to life.

I believe some of my strongest skills include:

  • Unity Engine
  • C# Programming
  • Version Control and merge conflict resolution
  • Unit testing
  • Communication and teamwork
  • Unreal Engine and blueprints
  • Tool development
